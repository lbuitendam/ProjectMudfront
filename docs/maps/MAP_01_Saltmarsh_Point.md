# Map 01 – "Saltmarsh Point" (original coastal map)

Functional targets come from `reference/map-study-seaside.md`. **The layout, landmarks and shapes are our own.** No BFH positions or terrain are used.

## Concept
A crooked headland with a **tidal salt marsh** in the middle, a **cliff-top windmill-lighthouse hybrid ("the Beacon Mill")** as the landmark, and a **fishing harbour** below it. Three teams come from three sides: the cliffs, the dunes and the farmland.

## Size and teams
- Play area **700 × 700 m** inside a 1024 m terrain (between Village 350 m and Seaside ~900 m; 30 players).
- 3 HQs ~**400 m** apart (triangle), each with high ground or distance as a trade-off:
  | Team | HQ | Height vs middle | Distance to marsh |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Tidewatch | Harbour quay (south) | −6 m (sea level) | 190 m (closest) |
  | Ironclads | Cliff fort (north-east) | +18 m | 240 m |
  | Muddogs | Dune farm (west) | +4 m | 215 m |

## Moving control zone: 5 zone locations (one active at a time)
| # | Name | Character | Radius |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Beacon Mill** | cliff top, landmark, 2 floors + balcony | 8 m |
| 2 | **Marsh Boardwalk** | wooden walkways over water, low cover, open | 10 m |
| 3 | **Net Sheds** | harbour warehouses, close range | 8 m |
| 4 | **Sunken Barge** | stranded ship on the mudflat, cover inside the hull | 9 m |
| 5 | **Dune Battery** | old artillery nests in the dunes | 9 m |
Neighbouring zones are **90–130 m** apart, so a relocation always means moving, but never too far.

## Routes (per HQ ≥ 2 infantry + 1 vehicle)
- **Vehicle ring road** around the marsh (gravel, 8 m wide) connects all HQs, with 2 bridges as choke points.
- **Infantry:** marsh boardwalks (short but exposed), dune trenches (covered, longer), cliff path (height advantage, narrow).
- Target times HQ → nearest zone: **≤ 25 s sprint**, ≤ 12 s by vehicle.

## Visual identity (our own)
- Palette: sandy beige + sea-green marsh + **orange-red roofs** of the fishing village + white-blue Beacon Mill.
- Dressing language: fishing nets, lobster pots, piers, wind-bent pines, reed beds (no BFH props).
- Night/storm variant later (rain, lighthouse beam).
